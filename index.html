JARVIS

A private, personal home assistant.

Jarvis runs on its owner's own computer at home. It lets that one household check on their PC and network, control their TVs, set reminders, and read and adjust their own Google Nest thermostat from their phone.

Jarvis is not a public service: there are no sign-ups, and it is used only by its owner. When it connects to a Google account, it does so only with that owner's explicit permission, and only to the devices they choose.
